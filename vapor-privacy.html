Privacy Policy

Vapor — Anesthesiology Reference App

Effective date: June 25, 2026

Overview

Vapor is a clinical reference application for anesthesiology. It is designed to work entirely offline. Your privacy is straightforward: we do not collect, store, or transmit any data.

Data Collection

Vapor does not collect any personal information, usage data, analytics, or device identifiers. The app does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any third-party analytics services.

Network Access

Vapor does not connect to the internet. All content is bundled within the app and available offline. No data is sent to or received from any server.

Third-Party Services

Vapor does not integrate with any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics platforms.

Data Storage

Any preferences you set within the app (such as accepting the disclaimer) are stored locally on your device using standard iOS mechanisms. This data never leaves your device.

Children's Privacy

Vapor is a professional medical reference tool. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
